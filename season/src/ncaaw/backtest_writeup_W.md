## Bottom line
Three rule families passed and go live: DOG_ML (strict), UNDER_DROP (lowered) and OVER (strict). Two college rules, MODEL_EDGE and NEUTRAL_UNDER, were approved Nov 2 and are live too. FAV_ML and SPREAD_MOVE stay off. Samples are small, so every pick grades LOW until the re-check builds a record.

## Data used
- 5,248 Division I games with an ESPN closing line: 3,100 in 2023-24, 1,222 in 2024-25, 926 in 2025-26.
- Same build as the men's card: ESPN scores, neutral and conference flags, AP rank, ESPN core odds, closing consensus as the median of listed books, team strength from games before each date blended by games / (games + 8).

## Data gaps
- ESPN carries lines for far fewer women's games in 2024-25 and 2025-26, so those seasons are thin and lean to Power conference games.
- Two opening books exist for only 891 games (all 2023-24). The rest use one book or none, so line move rules have few 2025-26 picks.
- No Action Network history (tickets, money, sharp signals); ticket conditions apply live only.
- No historical first half lines and no injury history.
- Ratings for some teams rest on very few lined games (for example Washington State on 3), so model projections are rough early.
- Every test is graded at the closing number.

## Rule families (three seasons, closing prices)
- DOG_ML strict (signals on the underdog and the line moved toward it): 54-67, +11.4u; 2023-24 +11.2u, 2024-25 +0.2u, 2025-26 no picks. Live, strict.
- UNDER_DROP lowered (total dropped at the books): 351-307 (53.3%), +12.1u, positive all three seasons. Strict was +6.3u. Live, lowered.
- OVER strict (total rose 2+): 195-176, +1.3u, positive in 2024-25 and 2025-26. Passes, but it is close to break-even. Live, flagged thin.
- FAV_ML: lowered -19.6u, strict -9.4u. Off.
- SPREAD_MOVE: lowered 813-879, -139.9u; strict -133.8u. Off.
- Model edge 3+ vs the spread: 1643-1452 (53.1%), +41.6u, positive in 2 of 3 seasons. The women's market looks softer than the men's.

## College tests
- Home court by conference: samples are small outside the SEC, ACC, Big Ten, Big 12 and Pac-12; none shows a usable edge (SEC +1.7u, ACC -47.3u).
- Early non-conference mismatches (spread 15+): favorites 459-477, underdogs 477-459, unders 419-520 (overs hit 55.4%).
- Conference vs non-conference: home ATS 49.5% and 51.0%; unders 52.1% in conference play (-12.4u), 48.6% outside it.
- Neutral sites: unders 515-403 (56.1%), +65.2u, positive all three seasons, mostly March (320-227). Holiday tournament underdogs 194-155, +21.4u, but only 2023-24 was positive.
- Rest and travel: no edge. The team with 2+ more days rest covered 50.1%; home teams against a road team on 1 day rest covered only 44.3% (-25.7u), the opposite of the men's result.
- Ranked vs unranked: unranked teams 51.7% ATS (-20.2u).
- Totals by pace tier: slow tier unders 53.1% (+15.3u) but positive only in 2023-24.
- First half lines: not tested (no history).

## Approved Nov 2, 2026
- MODEL_EDGE: side ATS when the projection beats the spread by 3+ points. 1643-1452, +41.6u, 2 of 3 seasons.
- NEUTRAL_UNDER: under at neutral sites. 515-403, +65.2u, 3 of 3 seasons, strongest in March.
- Both are live from Nov 2, 2026, and OVER (strict) stays live as approved.

## Backtest v2 (Nov 3, 2026): efficiency model and early season vs the opener
- Data: 22,282 games with box scores (2022-23 for the prior season, then 2023-24 to 2025-26). Lines exist for 2,956 games in 2023-24, 1,220 in 2024-25 and 922 in 2025-26. Same method and stricter bar as the men's card.
- The women's market is much softer than the men's. Most efficiency tests pass.
- Efficiency model at the close, spread edge 3+: 1029-804 (56.1%), +131.5u, positive all 3 seasons (+97.4u, +11.1u, +23.0u). Edge 5+: 492-363, +84.3u, 3 of 3.
- Efficiency overs 3+: 510-420 (54.8%), +43.6u, 3 of 3; most profit in Nov and Dec. Efficiency unders fail (1 of 3).
- Nov and Dec vs the opener: under 4+ below the open 219-158 (58.1%), +41.1u, 3 of 3; over 4+ above the open 233-173 (57.4%), +38.8u, 3 of 3; spread edge 3+ 579-482, +44.4u, 2 of 3.
- Data gaps: 2025-26 has opening totals for only 134 games, so the early season rules lean on 2023-24.
- Live from Nov 3, 2026: EFF_SPREAD, EFF_OVER, EARLY_UNDER_OPEN, EARLY_OVER_OPEN (all positive in 3 of 3 seasons). MODEL_EDGE stays live; when both fire, the rule with more season units makes the side pick.
